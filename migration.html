§ Early Human Migration to North America

The purpose of this map is to show the migration of peoples across the Bering Land strait and into North America. It was created for a panel entitled "Megafauna and Early Man," part of an exhibit installed in the nature center at Perrot State Park in Trempealeau, Wisconsin.
